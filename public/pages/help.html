COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Where do I find a club's application requirements?

Open the club from the clubs page. The club details show its current status, requirements, and application form when applications are available.

How do I check or edit an application I already submitted?

Go to My Forms and use the access details provided when you applied. Available actions depend on that application.

I did not receive a password recovery email. What should I do?

Check that you entered the email linked to the account and look in your spam folder. Password recovery depends on the site's email service being available. If it still does not arrive, contact your club through its official university channel.

Where can I ask about an event or my attendance?

Check the event information on the events page. For event-specific questions or an attendance correction, contact the organizing club through its official university channel.
